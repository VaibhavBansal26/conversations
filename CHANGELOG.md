### Changed

- Settings > AI agents creates a default agent (Aria, switched on) the first time it is opened in a workspace that has none.

### Added (calls with the AI assistant, teammate calls, call links)
- **Calls with the AI agent** ([docs/CALLS.md](docs/CALLS.md), [ADR 0033](docs/adr/0033-ai-calls-browser-speech.md)): visitors talk to the agent that answers their chat, by voice or by an animated-avatar video call, from the widget, the hosted chat and the mobile bridge. The visitor's browser does the speech (needs Chrome, Edge or Safari, not Firefox, and microphone permission; otherwise it falls back to the text chat), the server answers from the same pipeline as text. Captions, push-to-talk, barge-in, silence handling, language, a "Talk to a person" hand-over that keeps the visitor, a hard maximum duration, a daily budget, a concurrency cap and per-visitor limits. AI is disclosed at the start and in the UI; nothing is recorded; the visitor's camera is never sent. Off by default (`calls.ai`); agent actions `offer_call` (team or AI) and `ai_call`. Bridge events `ai_call_state` and `ai_call_unsupported`; support analytics gain an AI calls group.
- **Optional realtime voice engine** ([ADR 0035](docs/adr/0035-optional-realtime-voice-engine.md)): `calls.ai_engine = realtime` with `OPENAI_API_KEY` and `OPENAI_REALTIME_MODEL` (a short-lived secret per call, tools run by the server, a transcript monitor, minute budget, concurrency cap, kill switch, cost estimate, automatic fallback to the browser engine). **Not run against the real OpenAI service.**
- **Agent portraits, presets and the talking face** ([ADR 0034](docs/adr/0034-agent-portraits-and-the-talking-face.md)): upload a PNG, JPEG or WebP (1 MB, cropped, metadata stripped), alt text, the face (a photo with a level-driven glow, never lip-synced, or a drawn face), the AI label always visible, presets Aria and vb with two bundled portraits supplied by the repository owner, and a default Aria agent. Use only pictures you have the right to use.
- **Calls between teammates** ([ADR 0036](docs/adr/0036-teammate-calls.md)): one-to-one voice and video calls from a direct message or the member list, ringing anywhere in the app, respecting presence and do-not-disturb, with mute, camera, screen sharing, ICE restart, missed-call cards and the new `calls:internal` permission (owner, manager and agent). Off by default (`calls.internal`). Group calls are not built.
- **Call links** ([ADR 0037](docs/adr/0037-call-links.md)): an expiring, single-use, revocable link (128-bit token, only its hash stored) that opens `/call/<token>` with no login, for a call with the AI or the team, from the conversation menu ("Copy call link"), the AI's `share_call_link` action, or email or SMS with the contact's consent. The token never reaches logs, error reports or referrers; one generic message for every failure. A card with a Join button appears in the widget, the hosted chat and the bridge (`call_link_received`, `link_clicked` source `call_link`). Off by default (`calls.links`).
- REST: `GET /api/agents/presets`, `POST|DELETE /api/agents/:id/avatar`, `GET /api/team-calls/config`, `POST|GET /api/rooms/:id/call-links`, `DELETE /api/rooms/:id/call-links/:linkId`, `POST /public/call-links/preview|redeem`, `GET /call/:token`, `GET /avatars/:file`; socket events `aicall:*` and `tcall:*`. Migrations 036 to 040. Environment: `OPENAI_API_KEY`, `OPENAI_BASE_URL`, `OPENAI_REALTIME_MODEL`, `OPENAI_REALTIME_VOICE`, `OPENAI_REALTIME_TRANSCRIBE_MODEL`, `OPENAI_CLIENT_SECRET_TTL_SECONDS`, `AI_REALTIME_ENABLED`, `AI_REALTIME_COST_PER_MINUTE_USD`.
- Not verified: real speech quality and recognizers, Safari and Firefox, real devices and microphones, real NATs and TURN relays, real email or SMS delivery of links, any real language model, the real OpenAI realtime service.

### Added (phone calls)
- **Phone calls** ([docs/CALLS.md](docs/CALLS.md), [ADR 0032](docs/adr/0032-phone-calls-through-the-workspaces-twilio.md)): click-to-call through the workspace's own Twilio account. Twilio rings the team member's phone, then the contact's, from a caller ID chosen among the imported numbers; the thread keeps a card with the duration. Only contacts who agreed, on the callback form, to be called on that number are called; STOP and unsubscribed contacts never; countries must be allowed (nothing by default), premium-rate numbers are blocked, no calls at night in the contact's time zone, 10 calls per agent per hour, a daily limit per workspace, a dial time limit and ring time-outs.
- REST: `POST /api/rooms/:id/phone-call`, `/phone-call/consent`, `POST /api/calls/:id/hangup`, `PATCH /api/calls/settings`; webhooks `POST /webhooks/c/:id/twilio/voice/connect` and `/voice/status` (signature of the workspace's own token); `GET /api/calls/config` reports what is missing. The callback form now always asks for agreement to be called and sends the visitor's time zone. Settings > Calls gains the phone section. No migration.
- Not verified against real Twilio (mocked requests, signatures, TwiML and callbacks only).

### Added (browser calls)
- **Voice and video calls in the browser** ([docs/CALLS.md](docs/CALLS.md), [ADR 0031](docs/adr/0031-calls-signalling-over-sockets.md)): a team member rings a visitor who is in the chat; the visitor accepts or declines in the widget, the hosted chat or a mobile web view. WebRTC between the two browsers with mute, camera on/off, screen sharing, device choice, a call timer, a connection-quality indicator, reconnection after a network change (ICE restart) and an audio-only fallback; the thread keeps a card per call and a missed or declined call offers the visitor a callback form. Visitors can ask for a call ("Request a voice call") and an AI agent can offer one. Off by default per workspace; nothing is recorded.
- Signalling over the existing sockets (`call:invite`, `call:accept`, `call:offer`, `call:answer`, `call:ice`, `call:end`, `call:resume`, ...), a pure state machine with compare-and-set transitions, one live call per conversation, relay to the other party's socket only. TURN credentials per call: none (STUN), your own coturn (`TURN_URLS`, `TURN_SECRET`, `TURN_TTL_SECONDS`) or Twilio Network Traversal with the workspace's own account; `STUN_URLS`. Permissions `calls:make` (owner, manager, agent) and `calls:settings` (owner, manager) (37 permissions); `GET /api/calls/config`, `GET /api/rooms/:id/calls`; Settings > Calls; migrations 034 and 035, additive. Hosted-chat bridge events `call_requested` and `call_state`.
- Not verified: real NATs, a live TURN relay, Safari, Firefox, mobile browsers, real devices. Tested in two Chrome contexts with synthetic media.

### Added (visitor details)
- **Visitor details** ([docs/AI-AGENTS.md](docs/AI-AGENTS.md) section 4): Settings > Visitor details chooses, for name, email, phone and up to five custom fields, whether the chat never asks, asks and lets the visitor skip, requires, or asks when needed, and at which moment (a conversation goes to a person, nobody is available, a callback is requested, after it was resolved), with an optional consent checkbox and privacy link. A department can override the fields and moments. The widget and the hosted chat show a validated form card in the thread (phone to E.164 with a country picker, honeypot, skip, remembered and never re-asked), the AI agent can request details and a callback number, and an address that belongs to another contact is kept unverified instead of merged.
- REST: `POST /public/rooms/:id/forms/:messageId` and `/skip`; `GET /public/widget/config` returns `default_region` and `widget.visitor_info`; departments take `visitor_info`. Hosted-chat bridge events `form_requested`, `contact_info_submitted`, `handoff_started`, `agent_joined` (additive, protocol v1). Migration 033, additive.

### Added (AI support agents)
- **AI agents** ([docs/AI-AGENTS.md](docs/AI-AGENTS.md) section 3, [ADR 0029](docs/adr/0029-ai-agent-pipeline.md)): up to five agents per workspace answer visitors in the chat widget and hosted chat from the knowledge base (limited to chosen collections and departments, with hours), ask for details, and hand over to a person through the routing engine when the visitor asks, on a sensitive topic, for a VIP, when upset or urgent, after repeated rephrasing, at the turn limit, on low confidence or when nothing in the knowledge base fits. Answers carry the agent's name and an "AI" label, cite the knowledge-base page, and offer Yes / No / Talk to a person. Autonomy: suggest only (a private draft for the team), answer, or answer and resolve. A closed list of tools (`set_department`, `resolve_ticket`, ...) is validated, audited and shown to the team. Guards stop banned claims, ungrounded commitments, unsourced links and prompt injection. An offline rules agent answers when no model is configured, the model fails or the AI budget is used up.
- **Talk to a person** is always available in the widget (`POST /public/rooms/:id/request-human`), as are quick replies (`/quick-reply`), thumbs on answers (`/messages/:messageId/feedback`) and the rating card after a ticket is resolved (`/ticket-csat`). The widget and the hosted chat render them (five languages in the hosted chat); `GET /public/widget/config` returns `ai`.
- Team: take over a ticket from the agent (`claim`) and give it back (`POST /api/tickets/:id/hand-back`); rate answers in the conversation; Settings > AI agents with a sandbox ("try this agent": nothing is stored), a transcript review with ratings and a report of the questions the knowledge base could not answer; knowledge-base entries gain `collection` and `url`.
- REST: `/api/agents*` (CRUD, `/turns`, `/unanswered`, `/:id/sandbox`), `POST /api/messages/:id/feedback`. Permission `agents:manage` (35 permissions). Migrations 031 and 032, additive. Not verified against a real LLM; SMS and email agent replies and answer streaming are not built.

### Added (departments and routing)
- **Departments** ([docs/DEPARTMENTS.md](docs/DEPARTMENTS.md), [ADR 0028](docs/adr/0028-departments-are-inboxes.md)): every inbox is a department with hours and holidays (in its own time zone), a routing mode (round robin, least busy, skills based, manual pick; unconfigured inboxes keep their old behaviour), capacity per person, service levels, an overflow department, auto-replies, and members with skills, languages and their own capacity. Migrations 029 and 030, additive.
- **Handoff engine**: a ticket that needs a person is routed in one transaction under a per-workspace lock (no person is pushed over capacity, a ticket is assigned once, repeats are no-ops), queues when nobody can take it and is given out oldest first when a person becomes free, goes back through routing when its person goes away, escalates once when the first response is late, and can be transferred between departments with an internal note. Departments notify the person or the queue's team in-app and by the usual offline email.
- **Routing rules** (`/api/routing-rules`, a sandbox, Settings): the first matching rule on page address, language, channel, contact tag, email domain or contact attribute picks the department of a new conversation; a department the visitor picks wins. `POST /public/rooms` accepts `department_id`, `page_url` (origin and path kept) and `locale`; `GET /public/widget/config` lists the departments on offer with open and available flags and the new `widget.department_selector` setting.
- REST: `/api/departments`, `GET /api/department-status`, `/api/routing-rules*`, `POST /api/tickets/:id/claim` and `/transfer`. Permission `departments:manage` (34 permissions). Settings page "Departments & routing"; the ticket menu can take a waiting ticket and transfer it; the office-hours editor is now shared with the widget settings.

### Added (tickets, first part of AI support agents)
- **Tickets**: an episode of support inside a conversation, with a per-workspace number, status (`new`, `bot_handling`, `waiting_for_human`, `open`, `pending_customer`, `resolved`, `closed`), handler, department, priority, resolution, timestamps, rating, tags and a summary. Opened by the message path for every channel, reopened by a reply within `tickets.reopen_hours`, resolved by people, by closing the conversation or by an idle sweep, closed after `tickets.close_resolved_hours`; closing a ticket never closes the conversation. Messages carry `ticket_id`. See [docs/AI-AGENTS.md](docs/AI-AGENTS.md) and [ADR 0027](docs/adr/0027-tickets-inside-rooms.md).
- REST: `GET /api/tickets` (filters, keyset), `/counts`, `/:id`, `PATCH`, `/resolve`, `/close`, `/reopen`, `/merge`, `/bulk`, `GET /api/rooms/:id/tickets`, `GET /api/contacts/:id/tickets`, `POST /public/rooms/:id/ticket-csat`; the room list, room detail and room events carry `ticket`, and `GET /api/rooms` filters on `ticket_status`, `ticket_handler`, `department_id`. Socket event `ticket:updated`. Message events and exports gain optional `ticket_id` (additive).
- Permissions `tickets:read|write|close|merge` (33 permissions now); workspace settings `tickets.*`; migrations 027 and 028 (additive, no table rebuilt).
- Web: ticket chip and menu in the thread header, ticket history in the contact panel, a Tickets page with filters and bulk resolve/close, Settings > Tickets.

### Fixed (upgrading an existing SQLite database)
- **The server could not start on an existing SQLite database created by the previous release** (`table sqlite_master may not be modified`). Migration 020 widened the `messages.delivery` CHECK by editing `sqlite_master` with `writable_schema`, which SQLite builds in defensive mode (the Node image on Render) refuse. It now rebuilds `messages` with the documented procedure; the migration runner runs it with foreign keys off (`-- @sqlite-foreign-keys-off`) and fails, rolling back, on any new `foreign_key_check` violation, so `message_mentions` keeps every row. SQLite migrations now run one transaction each, and `SqliteDb` requests defensive mode. API, UI and `delivery_status` values are unchanged. A failed migration leaves the database at the previous version; see [RUNBOOK.md](docs/RUNBOOK.md#upgrading-an-existing-sqlite-database) and [ADR 0023](docs/adr/0023-sqlite-migrations-defensive-mode.md).
- New guards: upgrade tests that migrate a populated 019 database (SQLite and PostgreSQL), a schema-drift comparison with a fresh database, and a lint that forbids `writable_schema` / `sqlite_master` writes in migrations.

### Added (error monitoring)
- **Optional Sentry error monitoring** ([docs/MONITORING.md](docs/MONITORING.md), [ADR 0022](docs/adr/0022-optional-sentry-allow-list-scrubber.md)). Off unless `SENTRY_DSN` is set: with no DSN the SDK is not loaded and nothing is sent. Captures unhandled errors, 5xx responses, permanent job failures and socket handler errors, tagged with the route pattern, request or socket id, a salted hash of the workspace and its plan. Events are rebuilt from an allow-list, so request bodies, headers, cookies, query strings, users, message text, contact data, provider credentials and visitor or widget tokens are never sent; 4xx, validation, rate-limit, aborted and socket-disconnect errors are ignored. New variables `SENTRY_DSN`, `SENTRY_WEB_DSN`, `SENTRY_ENVIRONMENT`, `SENTRY_TRACES_SAMPLE_RATE` (0.05), `SENTRY_RELEASE`.
- The web app loads `@sentry/react` lazily from `GET /runtime-config.json` (one image works in every environment; initial JS is unchanged), with an error boundary and no session replay. `widget.js` never includes Sentry.
- Platform console: **Monitoring > Send a test error** (`POST /admin/api/monitoring/test`, superadmin).

### Added (per-workspace channel connections)
- **Settings > Channels: Connect Twilio and Connect SendGrid.** A workspace admin pastes their own credentials (encrypted at rest with `DATA_ENCRYPTION_KEY`, masked to the last four characters, never logged), verifies them with a read-only provider call, imports numbers from Twilio or verified senders from SendGrid, sends a test text or email, and copies the exact webhook URLs for Twilio (SMS webhook, status callback) and SendGrid (Inbound Parse, Event Webhook). ### Fixed
- Typing straight after picking an @mention no longer drops or misplaces characters (the caret is restored before paint instead of on a timer); room-list snippets show @mentions as names instead of raw `@[Name](id)` tokens.
- Rotating `JWT_SECRET` no longer breaks the identity pepper endpoint and the analytics exports (the pepper was encrypted under it).
- `npm run seed` exits when done (it stayed alive until killed); regression test spawns the script.
- `dow_created` (Sunday = 0), the `phone_key` extra field, the export page maximum (5,000) and how `Retry-After` relates to the `RateLimit` reset are now documented; the schemas say so.
- Event bus: after a consumer takeover the new owner now drains the previous owner's unacknowledged entries **before** reading new ones (per-key order could be violated); regression test added.
- Rate limiter: a 24-hour penalty no longer forgives itself; the Lua and TypeScript implementations agree.
- Webhook signature failures are counted from the response status (Hono turns thrown errors into responses).
